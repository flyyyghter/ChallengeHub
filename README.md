# ChallengeHub ◈

A sleek, modern liquid-glass collection of browser mini-games that test your precision, speed, arithmetic, and reaction. No installs, no accounts, just open and play.

## 🎮 Games

### 1. ⌨️ Typing Speed
Enter any text, a password, a phrase, a sequence of numbers, whatever you want. Timer starts on your first keypress and stops the moment you complete the last correct character. No Enter needed.

### 2. ⏱️ Timing Challenge (with 🙈 Blind Mode)
A random moment between 0 and 10 seconds is shown. You have to hit STOP exactly when the running timer reaches it.
- **Normal Mode:** Live clock with millisecond accuracy.
- **Blind Mode:** Digits hide as `?.??` and progress bar disappears right after launch — 100% pure internal rhythm feeling.
- 3 rounds per game with full deviation analytics, accuracy meter, and ranking.

### 3. 🧮 Math Rush
A 30-second rapid-fire mental arithmetic sprint!
- Solve rapid addition, subtraction, and multiplication problems.
- 4 liquid glass answer options with keyboard hotkeys (`1`, `2`, `3`, `4`).
- Multiplier combo streaks (`x1` to `x10`) for consecutive correct answers.
- Post-game breakdown: IQ score, accuracy %, max streak, and brain rank.

## ✨ Features
- 💧 **Ultra Liquid Glass UI:** Organic morphing fluid background orbs and water droplets with realistic specular reflections.
- ⚙️ **Settings Modal:** Dynamic live language switching (**RU / EN**) & sound volume slider with mute toggle.
- 🎵 **Tactile Sound FX:** Zero-latency audio feedback for clicks, correct hits, errors, and victory fanfares.
- ⌨️ **Full Keyboard Support:** Spacebar / Enter for Timing, keys `1-4` for Math Rush.
